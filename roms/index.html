Android ROMs

LineageOS for Microsoft and Nokia Lumia phones, which shipped with Windows and never had an Android vendor of their own.

Overview Lumia 950 XL Lumia 1520 Lumia 930 Apps and tools

Phones

  • Lumia 950 XL

    LineageOS 23.2, Android 16Available

    The first Android 16 build for the 950 XL: calls, LTE, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, NFC, both cameras, hardware video, USB-C video out and powered docks. VoLTE and Iris Unlock are experimental.

    Latest build: 11 November 2026

    Download Status and install guide

  • Lumia 1520

    LineageOS 18.1, Android 11Test builds

    Boots through BootShim and lk1st into Android. Wi-Fi, mobile data, SMS, NFC and hardware video already work; the microphone in calls, the camera and GPS are next.

    What works

  • Lumia 930

    LineageOS 18.1, Android 11Boots on a test phone

    Starts from the 1520 port, on the same chip. Android 11 now boots on a real Lumia 930; calls, sound and a public build come next.

    What works

Follow the 1520 and 930 ports on TelegramTest builds, progress and requests for testers come out there first.
