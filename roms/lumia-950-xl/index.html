The first Android 16 build for the Lumia 950 XL. The phone launched with Windows 10 Mobile and never had an Android vendor of its own, so this build runs a 3.10 kernel with BPF backports, the Android 13 Lumia blobs adapted to the Android 16 Treble linker, and HIDL wrappers for the radio and Wi-Fi.

LineageOS is a free, community-built Android distribution based on the Android Open Source Project, and it works without any Google apps. Treat this build as experimental.

New in build 2026-11-11

  • USB-C docks with their own power supply work: USB devices, Ethernet, video and charging through the dock.
  • The DisplayPort signal uses the values of the Windows driver and the Analogix reference, so the HD-500 dock trains at full speed.
  • No more crashes and freezes when you unplug a dock or while video is on.

What works

Display and system

  • Boot (non-A/B, the cache partition is used as /metadata)
  • WQHD AMOLED display with GPU acceleration, always-on display
  • Touch, double tap to wake
  • Performance profiles (Power save, Balanced, Performance) in Settings > Battery and in Quick Settings

Network and connections

  • Wi-Fi (QCA6174), also on phones without Wi-Fi and Bluetooth MAC provisioning
  • SIM, LTE data and SMS (IRadio 1.4 wrapper over rild)
  • Bluetooth, including A2DP audio streaming
  • NFC

Calls and sound

  • Voice calls (CSFB, 2G and 3G) with earpiece, microphone and speakerphone
  • Loudspeaker playback

Camera and video

  • Rear camera: autofocus, optical image stabilisation, correct colours, lower noise and brighter exposure; 1080p video at 30 fps with a new binned sensor mode
  • Front camera: colour photos and video, also in low light, with the colours of the Windows camera calibration
  • Rear LED flash and torch
  • Third-party camera apps: Open Camera (Camera1 and Camera2, manual ISO and shutter) and Arco
  • Video playback, YouTube with sound included, with hardware decoding and encoding (Venus firmware signed for this phone)

USB-C

  • ADB, automatic USB OTG
  • Charging up to 3 A with a USB-C charger; less current below 15 °C and no charging below 0 °C, as on Windows
  • Video out through simple USB-C to HDMI adapters, up to 1080p60
  • Docks with their own power supply: USB devices, Ethernet, video and charging. Tested with the Microsoft Display Dock HD-500, the ThinkPad USB-C Dock Gen 2 and a Switch-compatible dock

Sensors, storage and battery

  • Proximity (screen off during calls), accelerometer, gyroscope, magnetometer, light, barometer
  • MicroSD
  • Deep sleep with the screen off; the always-on display costs about 80 to 100 mA when enabled

Experimental

  • VoLTE (4G calling) through phh’s open-source IMS stack. It registers on the operator’s IMS network with the SIM’s authentication and IPsec, and carries the call audio itself. Tested on Iliad (Italy): outgoing calls with audio both ways, and SMS while VoLTE is on. Other carriers may work if their network accepts it: please report your carrier and the result. If mobile data stops after a dropped VoLTE call, reboot.
  • Iris Unlock (Settings > Security & privacy > Device unlock). The iris IR camera, its IR LED and enrolment work, with a live IR preview, but recognition doesn’t unlock the phone yet. It is a weak biometric, so banking apps and payments never accept it. The IR LED pulses in sync with the camera frames within the limits of the Windows driver, and the kernel switches it off 100 ms after the last write in any case.

Waiting for your reports

  • Calls through a Bluetooth headset (narrowband audio): fixed, waiting for confirmation
  • Battery life over long periods
  • VoLTE on carriers other than Iliad, and incoming VoLTE calls
  • Other USB-C docks and lapdocks: please report the model and what works

Not working yet

  • GPS: no satellite fix yet, under investigation on the modem side. With Google apps installed, network location (Wi-Fi and cell) works in Maps
  • Iris Unlock recognition
  • USB-C docks: the first connection can fail or restart the phone (plug it in again); the ThinkPad dock can’t charge the phone; the HP Elite x3 Lapdock gives video but no USB
  • SELinux is permissive

Install

A full install erases Windows 10 Mobile. Read the whole guide before you start. You flash at your own risk: there is no warranty, and a mistake can leave the phone unbootable until you recover it from fastboot.

With the installer (recommended)

The cityman installer for Windows follows the official Android4Lumia950 LK installer step by step (Developer Menu, boot shim, LK, Android partitions, provisioning), then installs this ROM with the Android 16 TWRP. The ROM needs that TWRP: its zip stops on the older one.

You need:

  • A Windows 10 or 11 PC
  • WPInternals, for a first install from Windows 10 Mobile
  • Android SDK Platform Tools (adb and fastboot) in your PATH
  • The Google USB driver, installed system-wide
  • The battery above 50% and a good USB cable
  • An ext4 cache partition, because the ROM uses it as /metadata. The ROM’s installer checks it and stops with instructions if it isn’t
  • For Clean and Update started from Android: USB debugging on
Installer modes
ModeStart fromWhat it does
Full Windows 10 Mobile, unlocked with WPInternals and in mass storage mode Erases Windows. Downloads the official Android4Lumia950 files it needs (about 110 MB), and backs up your EFIESP files and the 15 provisioning partitions to the PC before it erases anything. An interrupted full install can be resumed (see the installer’s README).
Clean LK and the Android partitions already in place, for example LineageOS 18.1 from Android4Lumia950 Installs this ROM and erases all data.
Update This ROM Installs the new build and keeps your data.
  1. Download the installer. Put the ROM zip and its SHA256SUMS in rom\, and twrp-cityman-a16.img in twrp\.
  2. Run installer.bat and choose Full, Clean or Update.
  3. Follow the prompts. Every destructive step asks you to type a confirmation word, every file is checked against its SHA-256 before it’s written, and the run is logged in logs\.
  4. Wait for the first boot: it takes several minutes.

The installer also fixes a bug in the official partition.sh, which could format the aboot partition (LK) while creating boot.

Manual installation

  1. Follow the official Android4Lumia950 guide: the installation video and the LK installer, cityman-temp branch.
  2. When the installer has rebooted to its TWRP and asks you to sideload a ROM, stop there. Don’t let it go on and flash its LineageOS 18.1 kernel.
  3. Reboot to the bootloader (fastboot) and boot the Android 16 TWRP: 
    fastboot boot twrp-cityman-a16.img
  4. First install only: Wipe > Format Data, then wipe Cache and Dalvik.
  5. Sideload the ROM from Advanced > ADB Sideload. SHA256SUMS is only needed by the installer. 
    adb sideload lineage-23.2-20261111-UNOFFICIAL-cityman.zip
  6. Optional: sideload an Android 16 GApps package (MindTheGapps or NikGapps core) before the first boot.
  7. Reboot.

Updating

Run the installer in Update mode, or boot the Android 16 TWRP again (step 3 above) and sideload the new zip without wiping data. If you use GApps, sideload them again right after the ROM, before you reboot.

If it bootloops

Get to fastboot, boot the Android 16 TWRP with fastboot boot and flash the ROM again, or run the installer in Clean or Update mode.

Extras

Arco

A camera app with a Lumia-style interface: arc dials for white balance, focus, ISO, shutter and exposure, and optional GCam-like multi-frame RAW processing on the GPU.
